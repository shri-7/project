THRESHOLD SENSITIVITY

01

Ingest & clean

Download or upload UCI Household Power Consumption data. Parse dates, convert invalid values, interpolate gaps, and aggregate to 15-minute intervals.

02

Chronological split

Use 70% for training, 15% for validation, and 15% for testing. Scale only on training data to prevent lookahead leakage.

03

Learn normal behavior

Create 96-step, 24-hour sequences and train an LSTM Autoencoder to reconstruct expected consumption patterns.

04

Detect anomalies

Calculate sequence mean absolute reconstruction error. The 99th validation percentile provides the default dynamic threshold.

05

Estimate wastage

Map anomalies to timeline points and add positive kW residuals multiplied by 0.25 hours. This is a statistical estimate, not proof of intent.

06

Benchmark

Compare against a 12-reading rolling mean plus 2.5 rolling standard deviations. Results remain unsupervised because ground truth is unavailable.
